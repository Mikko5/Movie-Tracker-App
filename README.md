# Movie Tracker App

Electron-based desktop application for tracking watched movies with TMDB search, custom metadata, and atomic data safety.

## Letterboxd Sync
You can automatically sync your newly watched movies straight from Letterboxd! Just save your username in the settings, and the app will sync up to your **last 50 reviewed movies** directly into your local tracker. It’s a great way to keep your local database up-to-date without double-entry.
